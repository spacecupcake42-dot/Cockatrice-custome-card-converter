PNG → Cockatrice XML

Add card images, fill in the card details, and download a ready-to-use Cockatrice custom set. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Set

Drop one or more card images here (PNG, JPG, WebP). Each image becomes a card, named from its filename.

Output


    

      
      
      
    

    

    

      The ZIP contains customsets/<SET>.xml and pics/<SET>/<Card Name>.png.
      Extract it into your Cockatrice data folder (the one that holds customsets and pics), then restart Cockatrice.
      Images are matched to cards by exact card name.
